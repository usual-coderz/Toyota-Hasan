A Teacher who sings

I am Talat — a dedicated teacher who believes every classroom is a stage and every lesson, a song. For me, education is not just books and boards; it is the art of igniting curiosity in young hearts.

Beyond the classroom, my true melody lives in singing. Whether it's a soulful ghazal or a cheerful folk tune, music is where my spirit finds its rhythm — and often, my students get to hear a little of it too!
